The Art of the Heart with Salim Najjar

Listen on Apple Podcasts

Naeem talks with Salim Najjar, known as That HRV Guy, about heart rate variability and the language of the nervous system. Salim's work helps people understand HRV as a practical signal for recovery, performance, and emotional regulation. The episode title, The Art of the Heart, points to treating heart-rate data as something you can train with, not just track.

Key takeaways

  1. HRV is a useful signal for how the nervous system is coping with load.
  2. High performers can train awareness around recovery instead of only pushing harder.
  3. Salim frames HRV education as a language people can learn and use day to day.
  4. His public work spans podcasts, coaching, and practical nervous-system education.

About Salim Najjar

Salim Najjar (That HRV Guy) teaches heart rate variability and nervous-system awareness for performance and recovery. He has appeared on shows including The Dr. Hyman Show, Biohacking Bestie, The Meat Mafia, and Extend Podcast.

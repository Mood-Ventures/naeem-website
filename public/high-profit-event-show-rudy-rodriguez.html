Naeem joined Rudy Rodriguez on The High Profit Event Show, from Virtual Event Sales Team (V.E.S.T.), for Episode 70, Maximizing Event Impact. Rudy introduces Naeem as CEO of Mood Ventures, a peak performance coach and former Tony Robbins speaker. The conversation is about what makes an event land for the people in the room, and what makes that event profitable.

They talk about emotional state and decisions. Naeem's point, as the episode describes it, is that people make different decisions when they are in a strong state. The moment you present an offer or a next step is part of the event, not an afterthought. He talks about meeting the room when people are most open.

They also talk about selling as service. If you believe the work helps, making the offer is a way to take care of the client. Naeem describes paying attention to each person there, in a small group or a large one, and how that care changes the way people take part.

The last part is how to keep a room with you. Naeem talks about energy from the first interaction through the final session, and about making the event something people join rather than only watch. He gives movement, including dance, as one way he keeps a room present.

Key takeaways

  1. The moment you ask for a decision is part of the event. State shapes what people are willing to do next.
  2. Present the offer or the next step when the room is open, not only because the agenda says it is time.
  3. If you believe the work helps, the offer is a way to serve. Attention to each person builds trust.
  4. Energy and participation have to last from the opening interaction to the last session.
  5. Movement, including dance, is one way Naeem keeps a room awake and involved.

About Rudy Rodriguez

Rudy Rodriguez hosts The High Profit Event Show and founded Virtual Event Sales Team, which works with thought leaders, brands, and companies on virtual events. He spent 10 years in the Navy. After that he trained with a coach and mentor, then worked as a sales coach, before starting Virtual Event Sales Team. On YouTube the episode is published under the name Rodolfo Rodriguez.

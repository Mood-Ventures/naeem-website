About Naeem Mahmood, Former Tony Robbins National Speaker

Naeem Mahmood is a keynote speaker, executive coach, and former Tony Robbins national speaker, known for his work on peak performance, leadership, and identity.

Before he ever stepped on a stage, Naeem built a career by every conventional measure of success: a college basketball career, an NYU cum laude degree, a job on Wall Street, and a move into Silicon Valley. Then he attended a Tony Robbins event that changed the trajectory of his life. Within a year, he had left his career to join Tony Robbins Companies as a national speaker and trainer.

Over five years, Naeem delivered 1,000 talks and impacted the lives of 50,000+ people across the United States and internationally. He worked directly with high performers, executives, founders, and operators, helping them see that the gap between where they are and where they want to be is rarely about more information. It’s about identity, biology, and the relationships they’ve been managing instead of inhabiting.

Naeem is an ICF-accredited coach, NLP trainer, and hypnotist, certified through Performance Coach University. He is the creator of Peak Human Protocol, a private coaching framework built on three pillars: Peak Mind, Peak Body, and Peak Love. He also hosts the Peak Humans podcast, where he interviews founders, executives, and high performers about the inner work behind their outer success.

As a keynote speaker, Naeem doesn’t deliver motivational soundbites. His talks are identity interventions, designed to shift how leaders see themselves and operate in the world, whether the room is a founder community, an executive leadership offsite, a sales kickoff, or an entrepreneurial conference. Event organizers book Naeem when they want their audience to leave not just inspired, but different. Read his full story or recent writing on the blog.

Naeem splits his time between Los Angeles, Medellín, and Puerto Rico, and is available for keynotes, workshops, and executive retreats worldwide, in person or virtually.

Sales Keynote Speaker FAQ

What topics does Naeem speak on?

Peak Performance Selling: the psychology of closing, state management, and the belief systems that drive sales execution. His flagship keynote and talk formats are listed above.

Who is Naeem Mahmood as a sales keynote speaker?

Naeem Mahmood is a former Tony Robbins national speaker. He is an ICF-accredited coach, NLP trainer and hypnotist, and he hosts the Peak Humans podcast. His keynote, Peak Performance Selling: The Psychology of Closing, covers the belief systems and emotional states that shape how a sales team performs.

How is Naeem different from a typical sales motivational speaker?

Most sales trainings focus on scripts, process and tactics. Naeem focuses on the operating system underneath: psychology, state management and belief systems. The goal is for your team to execute differently after the event, not just feel inspired for a day.

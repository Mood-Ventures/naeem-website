About Naeem.

Naeem Mahmood is a keynote speaker, executive coach, and former national speaker and trainer for Tony Robbins Companies, one of the most demanding speaking environments in the world.

Before he ever stepped on a stage, Naeem built a career by every conventional measure of success: a college basketball career, a cum laude degree from NYU, a job on Wall Street, and a move into Silicon Valley. Then he attended a Tony Robbins event that changed the trajectory of his life. Within a year, he had left his career to join Tony Robbins Companies as a national speaker and trainer.

Over five years, Naeem delivered more than 1,000 talks to over 50,000 people across the United States and internationally. He worked directly with high performers, executives, founders, and operators, helping them see that the gap between where they are and where they want to be is rarely about more information. It’s about identity, biology, and the relationships they’ve been managing instead of inhabiting.

Naeem is an ICF accredited coach, NLP trainer and hypnotist, and certified through Performance Coach University. He is the creator of Peak Human Protocol, a private coaching framework built on three pillars: Peak Mind, Peak Body, and Peak Love. He also hosts the Peak Humans podcast, where he interviews founders, executives, and high performers about the inner work behind their outer success.

As a keynote speaker, Naeem doesn’t deliver motivational soundbites. His talks are identity interventions, designed to shift how leaders see themselves and operate in the world, whether the room is a founder community, an executive leadership offsite, a sales kickoff, or an entrepreneurial conference. Event organizers book Naeem when they want their audience to leave not just inspired, but different.

Naeem splits his time between Los Angeles, Medellín, and Puerto Rico, and is available for keynotes, workshops, and executive retreats worldwide, in person or virtually.

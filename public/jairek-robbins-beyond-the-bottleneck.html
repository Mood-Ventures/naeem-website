Beyond the Bottleneck with Jairek Robbins

Listen on Apple Podcasts

Naeem sits down with Jairek Robbins, entrepreneur, performance coach, speaker, and founder of Executive Office AI. For more than 20 years Jairek has advised founders, CEOs, and leadership teams across 137 countries. He is the author of the bestselling book LIVE IT!, and his family was the first ever featured on the cover of SUCCESS Magazine. After two decades of high-level coaching, he came to believe the biggest obstacle to peak performance is not only mindset. It is what leaders spend their time and energy on. Today he is building AI-powered tools to give business owners world-class strategic support at scale.

Key takeaways

  1. Founders often become their own bottleneck by staying trapped in day-to-day work.
  2. Mindset matters, and so does where a leader puts time and energy.
  3. Strategy, execution, leadership, and performance have to strengthen together.
  4. AI can extend strategic support when it is built around real executive bottlenecks.
  5. Jairek's own show, Beyond the Bottleneck, continues the same theme for founders and CEOs.

About Jairek Robbins

Jairek Robbins is a performance coach, keynote speaker, founder of PerformAnce Coach University, and founder of Executive Office AI. He has coached business owners and teams for more than two decades and is the author of LIVE IT!.

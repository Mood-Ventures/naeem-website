Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from coaching?

1:1 coaching is deep, custom, and high-touch, $3–5K/month. SuperMood Daily is community-driven accountability with weekly group access to me. Different offer for a different need.

What if I miss a day?

It happens. But the group notices. That’s the point.

What platform is this on?

Private group on a messaging platform. Details after you join.

Can I cancel?

Yes, after your minimum commitment (3 months for monthly, 1 year for annual). Founding members are lifetime rate but not lifetime commitment. You can leave anytime.

How does the referral program work?

Refer someone who joins and you get your next month free. They get 20% off their first month. No cap. Just tell them to mention your name.

Will you add more spots?

Yes, but founding member rate is capped at the first 10. After that, everyone pays $250/mo.

About Naeem

From Wall Street to the Tony Robbins stage.

Naeem built a career by every conventional measure of success: college basketball, an NYU cum laude degree, Wall Street, then Silicon Valley. Then he attended a Tony Robbins event that changed the trajectory of his life, and he joined Tony Robbins Companies as a national speaker and trainer.

He has delivered more than 1,000 talks to over 50,000 people. Today he speaks to sales teams and leaders and coaches a small number of high performers. He also hosts the Peak Humans podcast.

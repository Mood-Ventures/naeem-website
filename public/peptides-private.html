The “US-Made” Deception

Most suppliers claiming “US-made” peptides are lying. Here’s how it works:

The Common Playbook

  1. Import peptides from China
  2. Repackage in US warehouse
  3. Slap “Made in USA” on the label
  4. Send to US lab for testing (making the COA technically “US”)

Technically legal. Completely misleading.

When you see “US-made” from most suppliers, they mean the vial was filled here. The peptide inside? Made overseas, then imported.

Our Manufacturing Process

01

Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS)

We build peptides amino acid by amino acid using SPPS in climate-controlled cleanrooms. Every coupling reaction is monitored in real-time.

02

Purification

Crude peptides undergo HPLC purification to achieve >99% purity. We don’t ship anything below our standard.

03

Lyophilization & Sterile Fill

Peptides are freeze-dried and filled in sterile vials under ISO Class 5 conditions. Same standards as pharmaceutical manufacturing.

04

Third-Party Testing

Every batch goes to independent labs (Eurofins, SGS, Charles River) for verification. Full COAs published before sale.

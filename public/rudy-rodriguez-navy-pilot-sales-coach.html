Insights from a Navy Pilot Turned Sales Coach with Rudy Rodriguez

Listen on Apple Podcasts

In this Peak Humans episode, Naeem talks with Rudy Rodriguez, a former Navy aviator turned sales coach. Rudy shares the path from military service into entrepreneurship, including the transition after he left the Navy, what personal growth asked of him, and how his view of peak performance has changed. He talks about integrity, the place of peer groups, and personal suffering as part of finding more of what a person can do.

Key takeaways

  1. Rudy walks through the move from Navy service into building a business, including the transition after he left.
  2. Personal growth and a changing view of peak performance are part of that story.
  3. He puts weight on integrity and on peer groups.
  4. He also talks about personal suffering as part of finding more of what a person can do.
  5. His sales work is a systematic, consultative approach to enrollment, and that is how he builds sales teams.

About Rudy Rodriguez

Rudy Rodriguez is a Navy pilot turned sales coach, with over $50 million in sales of coaching, education, and mastermind offers since 2015. He is a former Naval officer and aviator. He uses a systematic, consultative approach to enrollment and sales, and he builds A-player sales teams. He founded Virtual Event Sales Team.

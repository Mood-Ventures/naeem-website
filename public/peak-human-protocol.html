Peak Human Protocol

A private 12-month container for founders ready to build a life that matches what they’ve built.

Apply for a Breakthrough Call

Most high performers are trying to fix the wrong thing.

They optimize their calendar when the issue is their identity. They hire a business coach when the issue is their marriage. They chase more revenue when the issue is that nothing they build makes them feel alive.

I spent a decade watching this pattern. First on Wall Street, then in Silicon Valley, then on Tony Robbins’ stages with tens of thousands of people. The men and women who had “made it” were the ones sitting in my office asking why they still felt empty.

Peak Human Protocol is what I built for them.

The Premise

Peak performance is not a strategy problem.

It’s an integration problem.

You already know what to do. You’ve read the books. You’ve hired the coaches. You’ve built the systems. What you’re missing is not more information. It’s the alignment of three pillars most people never work on together.

When these three are integrated, everything you touch works. When they’re not, you can win the game on paper and still feel like you’re losing.

The Three Pillars

01

Peak Mind

Identity, leadership, money. Who you are underneath the performance.

Most successful people are still operating from the identity of the person who had to achieve. The striver, the outsider, the one who had something to prove. That identity got you here. It will not take you where you want to go next.

The work starts with dismantling the story of who you had to be, and installing the identity of who you are now. This is where we rebuild your relationship with leadership, decision-making, sales, and money from the inside out.

02

Peak Body

Biology, longevity, peptides. How long you get to live this.

The body is the foundation. If the biology is broken, no amount of mindset work sticks.

This is where I go deep on sleep, nutrition, movement, hormones, peptides, and diagnostics with my medical team. Not as a wellness trend, but as the infrastructure that lets your nervous system hold everything else.

You cannot lead a life of meaning inside a body running on fumes.

03

Peak Love

Relationships, marriage, presence. What you actually live in.

Marriage. Family. Friendship. The people you share your life with. You can be a titan in business and a stranger at your own dinner table. Peak Human Protocol treats love as a discipline. Not a byproduct of success, but a skill that requires the same intentionality you brought to your career.

If biology is the foundation and identity is the frame, love is what you actually live in.

What’s Included

Peak Human Protocol is a 12-month private container. High-touch. Direct access. Built for the founder ready to integrate all three pillars into one operating standard.

  • 1:1 coaching with Naeem across Peak Mind, Peak Body, and Peak Love
  • Advanced diagnostics and lab panels with my medical team
  • Personalized peptide protocol via Frame Longevity
  • Ongoing text and voice access between sessions
  • Quarterly in-person immersions
  • Full integration across identity, biology, and relationships

How It Works

This isn’t a course. It isn’t a program you complete.

It’s a living container I run with every client. We assess where you are across the three pillars, identify the one that’s holding the others back, and go to work.

For some people, biology is the bottleneck. For others, it’s a story about identity they’ve been carrying for twenty years. For most, it’s love. The relationships they’ve been managing instead of inhabiting.

Peak Human Protocol meets you where you are. And then it takes you where you’re ready to go.

Why It Works

Because it treats you as a whole system.

Every other coaching framework picks a lane (business, health, or relationships) and pretends the other lanes don’t matter. They do. And when you work on one without the others, you get short-term gains that collapse under the weight of everything you haven’t addressed.

Peak Human Protocol is what I use with founders running eight and nine-figure companies. It produces the kind of results that don’t fade, because they’re built on all three pillars at once.

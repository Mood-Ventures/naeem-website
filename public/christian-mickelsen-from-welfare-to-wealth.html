From Welfare to Wealth with Christian Mickelsen

In this Peak Humans episode, Naeem sits down with Christian Mickelsen, a coach and author whose work focuses on helping coaches get clients and build businesses that create real change. Christian grew up on welfare outside Chicago, found coaching as a client when his own life was stuck, and built a coaching company that has made the Inc. 5000 list four years in a row. The conversation centers on how coaching can move someone from scarcity to contribution, and why getting more of the world coached is the mission behind his work.

Key takeaways

  1. Coaching can be the turning point when work, relationships, health, and business all feel stuck at once.
  2. Early life struggle with money and conflict can become fuel for a lifelong commitment to personal growth.
  3. A clear mission (get the whole world coached) gives shape to both books and business systems.
  4. Helping other coaches get clients multiplies impact further than coaching alone.
  5. Published books such as Abundance Unleashed and Get Clients Today are part of how Christian packages what he teaches.

About Christian Mickelsen

Christian Mickelsen is a personal development and coaching authority and the author of five books, including Abundance Unleashed and Get Clients Today. He is the founder and president of a coaching business that has made Inc. 5000's fastest growing companies list four years in a row. A coach for 25 years, he is on a mission to get the whole world coached.
